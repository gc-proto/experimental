Cutting hours instead of cutting jobs

Work-Sharing lets you cut hours instead of cutting jobs. The WRRP will replace and expand it, with up to $1,000 per participating employee toward training costs.

  • Work-Sharing Program (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Worker Retention Grant (requires an approved Work-Sharing agreement) (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC

You already have a Work-Sharing agreement

Agreements now run from 4 to 152 weeks and hours can be shared in different proportions among employees.

  • Worker Retention Grant (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Training options while on Work-Sharing (Training Finder and other resources) (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
  • Work-Sharing Program: agreement terms and flexibilities (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC

Training and upskilling your current employees

Training happens in the hours your employees are not working, so you keep the skills you have built while you transform, retool or diversify.

  • Workforce Retention and Retraining Program (WRRP): up to $1,000 per participating employee (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Workforce Tariff Response - EI-funded LMDAs (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Training options for employers (Training Finder) (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank

Recruiting and hiring new workers

  • Adapt to today's marketplace using Job Bank: find, hire and retain workers (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
  • Find workers with the right skills: available workers by occupation (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
  • Student Work Placement Program: wage subsidies to hire post-secondary students (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
