SUMMARY
-------
The live wizard asks four questions (need > region > revenue > sector) and
reveals matching program panels. Its "Workforce retention and retraining"
branch ran through the same region/revenue/sector chain as the financing
branches and ended in a single generic panel of four links.

That branch has been replaced with a dedicated employer path of five
questions: need > workforce situation > region > revenue > sector. The
workforce-situation question is multi-select, because employers commonly
face more than one of these situations at once, and each ticked situation
opens its own panel of supports.

Every employer support listed under the "Employer supports" heading on the
businesses-industries page, plus the three programs described on the
Workforce Retention and Retraining Program (WRRP) page, is now reachable.
All nine destination URLs were checked and return HTTP 200.


CHANGES IN ORDER
----------------

1. <base> element and provenance comment added to <head>
   Added immediately after the opening <head> tag:
     <base href="https://www.canada.ca/en/campaign/canadastrong/
                 find-support-for-your-business.html"/>
   The page's own relative asset and link URLs (WET/GCWeb CSS and JS, the
   /en/... program links) therefore resolve against canada.ca when the file
   is opened from disk. This matches the convention already used by
   eric/services.html and eric/sign-in-online-account.html.

2. Intro sentence extended
   Was: "Answer four questions and we will show you the programs that fit
        your need, region, size and sector."
   Now: same sentence, plus "If you are an employer looking to keep, retrain
        or hire workers, we will add a question about your workforce
        situation, where you can select as many situations as apply."

3. Question 1 prompt no longer names a total
   Was: "Question 1 of 4: What do you need right now?"
   Now: "Question 1: What do you need right now?"
   Question 1 is shared by every branch and the branches are no longer all
   the same length (four questions, or five on the employer branch), so the
   total is not known until the need is chosen. Questions 2 onward still
   name their branch total: "Question 3 of 4" on the financing, liquidity,
   transformation and "All of the above" branches, "Question 3 of 5" on the
   employer branch.

4. Question 1, fourth option relabelled and re-pointed
   Label  : "Workforce retention and retraining"
         -> "Workforce: keeping, retraining or hiring workers"
   Actions: still reveals #p-workforce-agnostic, but now appends
            #q2-need-emp instead of #q2-need-wrk.
   The other four options of question 1 are untouched.

5. Retired question chain deleted (13 <div> blocks)
   Removed as unreachable once question 1 re-points:
     #q2-need-wrk
     #q3-need-wrk-reg-atl | -qc | -on-n | -on-s | -pr | -bc | -nor
     #q4-need-wrk-size-under1m | -1to5m | -5mplus | -large | -nonprofit
   These were near-duplicates of the financing/liquidity/transformation
   chains and produced no workforce-specific output.

6. Four new question blocks added (before #q2-need-all)
     #q2-need-emp  What is your workforce situation right now?  MULTI-SELECT
     #q3-need-emp  Where is your business primarily located?    (7 answers)
     #q4-need-emp  What is your annual revenue?                 (5 answers)
     #q5-need-emp  What is your sector?                         (4 answers)
   All five situations append the same #q3-need-emp, all seven regions
   append the same #q4-need-emp, and all five revenue bands append the same
   #q5-need-emp. This is the pattern the original page already uses for
   #q4-need-fin-size-under1m, which is appended by all seven financing
   region blocks. Thirteen deleted blocks are replaced by four.

7. #q2-need-emp is rendered as checkboxes, not radios
   "renderas":"checkbox" on the question config. Two consequences handled:

   a. The append that renders question 3 sits in the question's own
      "default" action list rather than on each answer. fieldflow runs
      "default" once per change event but runs an answer's actions once per
      selected answer, so leaving the append on the answers would have
      appended question 3 once per box ticked.

   b. Ticking or unticking a box re-runs the question, which tears down
      questions 3 to 5 and collapses the results. That is the same reset
      behaviour the radio questions already have, and it is correct - a new
      situation can change what the later answers should surface - but it
      does mean an employer who adds a situation late has to re-answer the
      questions below it. The prompt says "Select all that apply" to push
      the selections to the front.

8. #p-workforce-agnostic rewritten
   Was a four-link catch-all panel titled "Workforce retention and
   retraining: open to all sectors". It is revealed by question 1, before
   the situation is known, so it is now the two-link panel that is true for
   every employer regardless of situation:
     - Workforce Retention and Retraining Program (WRRP)   [NEW]
     - Support for workers impacted by tariffs             [NEW here]
   Its three former links (Work-Sharing, Worker Retention Grant, Workforce
   Tariff Response) moved into the situation panels below, where they are
   shown with the situations they actually apply to. Student Work Placement
   moved to the hiring panel.

9. Five situation panels added (new, class "wz-g wz-g-emp")
     #p-emp-reduce     Cutting hours instead of cutting jobs
     #p-emp-agreement  You already have a Work-Sharing agreement
     #p-emp-train      Training and upskilling your current employees
     #p-emp-hire       Recruiting and hiring new workers
     #p-emp-layoff     If you have laid off workers or expect to
   Each carries two or three links. Because the question is multi-select,
   several of these can be on screen at once, so each programme is listed
   in the one or two panels where it is genuinely the answer rather than in
   every panel it touches. The reduce, agreement and train panels also
   carry a one-sentence lead paragraph drawn from the WRRP page (the $1,000
   per-employee training amount, the 4-to-152-week agreement range,
   training in non-working hours).

10. Size panel added
    #p-emp-size-note  Based on the size of your business
    A panel-default section with three list items, only one of which is
    visible at a time:
      under $1M / $1-5M / $5M+   WRRP delivered with the regional
                                 development agencies for SMEs
      $150M+                     larger enterprises deal directly with
                                 Service Canada for Work-Sharing
      non-profit                 eligible employer under the Student Work
                                 Placement Program

11. New CSS hook: .wz-emp-size-*
    Question 4 of the employer branch reveals .wz-emp-size-under1m (and so
    on) rather than the existing .wz-sz-size-under1m. The existing size
    classes are revealed by question 3 of every other branch, so reusing
    them would have made #p-emp-size-note appear in the financing, liquidity
    and transformation results as well. The employer panels still carry
    wz-g wz-g-sz so the existing reset actions hide them.

12. Sector question reuses the existing sector hubs
    #q5-need-emp offers the same four sectors as the other branches and
    reveals the same #hub-forestry-and-lumber, #hub-steel-and-aluminum and
    #hub-agriculture panels. "Other manufacturing and exporting" reveals no
    hub, as on the other branches. No new sector panels were needed: none
    of the employer supports vary by sector, but the sector hubs carry
    employer-relevant material, in particular NRCan's "Support for forest
    sector employers and workers".

13. #ap-workforce-agnostic expanded (the "All of the above" path)
    That path never asks the situation question, so its workforce panel now
    lists all nine employer supports: WRRP, Work-Sharing, Worker Retention
    Grant, Workforce Tariff Response, Student Work Placement, Job Bank
    hiring resources, Job Bank available-workers dashboard, Job Bank
    training options, and Support for workers impacted by tariffs.
    Title changed to "Workforce retention, retraining and hiring: open to
    all employers".

14. Click-analytics script rewritten
    The original script hard-coded QUESTIONS = 4, collected only
    input[type='radio']:checked, and fired when exactly four radios were
    checked. All three assumptions break on a five-question branch whose
    second question is multi-select. Replaced with:

    - End-of-branch detection instead of a question count. fieldflow
      stamps data-wb-fieldflow-source="<source div id>" onto every fieldset
      it renders and leaves the hidden source <div> in the document, so the
      script looks the source up and treats the question as the end of its
      branch when neither the question config nor any of its answers
      contains an "append" action. No list of terminal questions to
      maintain: adding or reordering questions needs no script change.
    - Answer collection walks the fieldsets in order and joins each
      question's checked controls, so a multi-select question contributes
      one entry. Multiple answers to one question are joined with " + ",
      keeping " > " as the between-questions separator.
    - The listener now matches checkboxes as well as radios, and bails if
      any question on screen has no answer.

    Sample paths produced, both captured in-browser:
      ServCan:CanadaStrongWizard:Liquidity for a tariff-driven cash-flow
      gap > Quebec > Larger enterprise ($150 million or more) > Steel,
      aluminium and copper

      ServCan:CanadaStrongWizard:Workforce: keeping, retraining or hiring
      workers > I need to reduce employee hours instead of laying people
      off + I need to recruit and hire new workers > British Columbia >
      Under $1 million > Agriculture, agri-food, fish and seafood

15. "Start Over" link re-pointed
    From /en/campaign/canadastrong/find-support-for-your-business.html to
    canada-strong-employer-wizard.html, so restarting stays in the mockup
    instead of jumping to the live page.

KNOWN GAPS / SUGGESTED NEXT STEPS
---------------------------------
- Ticking an extra situation after reaching the results costs the employer
  their region, revenue and sector answers. Preserving them would mean
  moving the multi-select question to the end of the branch, or holding
  state outside fieldflow.
- Nothing stops an employer ticking both "I need to reduce employee hours
  instead of laying people off" and "I already have a Work-Sharing
  agreement in place", which are close to mutually exclusive. The two
  panels overlap by one link (Worker Retention Grant) if they do.
- Question totals are hard-coded in the prompt text, as on the live page.
  Any change to a branch's length means editing those strings, though the
  analytics script no longer needs to be touched.
- WRRP is described on its own page as a future program. The wording in
  the panels says so; it will need revisiting when the program launches
  and the Work-Sharing and Worker Retention Grant pages are superseded.


REVISION 2 (2026-09-10) — what changed from the first draft
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Sector question added back as question 5 of the employer branch, and the
  employer branch renumbered from "of 4" to "of 5". Question 1's prompt
  dropped its total. (items 3, 6, 12)
- The two overlapping situations were reworded to separate current
  employees from new hires:
    "I need to retrain or upskill the workers I have"
      -> "I need to train or upskill my current employees"
    "I need to find, hire or keep the right workers"
      -> "I need to recruit and hire new workers"
  The word "keep" left the hiring option, since retention is what the
  first two situations are about. Panel titles followed: "Retraining and
  upskilling the workers you have" -> "Training and upskilling your
  current employees", and "Finding, hiring and keeping the right workers"
  -> "Recruiting and hiring new workers". Workforce Tariff Response left
  the hiring panel, where it had been justified as training for new hires,
  and now appears only in the training panel.
- The situation question became multi-select. (item 7)
- Panel contents were thinned so that two or three panels on screen at once
  stay readable: WRRP now appears in the always-on panel and the training
  panel rather than in four panels; the Job Bank training-options link was
  removed from the reduce panel, where it did not belong, since it is for
  employers who already hold an agreement; the available-workers dashboard
  was removed from the layoff panel. (item 9)
- The analytics script was rewritten, since a five-question branch with a
  multi-select question broke every assumption in the original. (item 14)


REVISION 3 (2026-09-10) — French version added
-----------------------------------------------
eric/un-canada-fort-assistant-employeurs.html is the French wizard, built
from the live French page with exactly the same structural edits as the
English one, items 1 to 15 above. Same ids, same classes, same fieldflow
actions, same analytics script - the French page on canada.ca mirrors the
English one node for node, so nothing had to be reshaped for it.

The builder was refactored to do this. It is one script with one set of
structural edits and two language packs; every string that differs between
the two files lives in a dictionary, so the two can no longer drift apart
structurally. Rebuilding produced a byte-for-byte equivalent English file
apart from the two changes in this revision.

French wording was taken from the French Government of Canada pages, not
back-translated from the English mockup:
  - Programme names and URLs come from the French wizard's own workforce
    panel (Travail partagé, Subvention pour le maintien à l'emploi des
    travailleurs, Réponse tarifaire pour la main-d'oeuvre, Programme de
    stages pratiques pour étudiants), from entreprises-industries.html
    #emp-sup (the three Guichet-Emplois links and Soutien aux travailleurs
    en réponse aux droits de douane), and from the PMERMO page.
  - Region, revenue and sector answer labels were lifted verbatim from the
    French wizard's existing questions, including its abbr markup.
  - The 1 000 $ figure, the 4-to-152-week range and the regional-development
    -agency delivery sentence are the French page's own phrasing.
  - New-window text is the French page's "(Ouvre l'emplacement dans un
    nouvel onglet.)".

Two French-specific notes:
  - The French Q1 prompt had irregular spacing in the source ("Question  1
    sur 4  :  De quoi..."). Rewriting it to drop the total also normalised
    it to "Question 1 : De quoi...".
  - entreprises-industries.html#emp-sup writes "Guichet-Emploi" in one link
    title while its own body text and the site itself use "Guichet-Emplois".
    The mockup uses "Guichet-Emplois" rather than carrying the typo across.

Also in this revision, in both files: the header language toggle now points
at the sibling mockup instead of the live page, so the pair can be reviewed
as a pair. Everything else in the header, footer and breadcrumb is
untouched.

All nine French destination URLs return HTTP 200. The French file was
walked in a browser the same way as the English one: the five-question
employer branch, multi-select with two situations ticked (both panels
shown, question 3 appended once), size gating on the non-profit answer,
the sector question revealing the forestry hub, and the four-question
liquidity branch as a regression check. The analytics path it produces:

  ServCan:CanadaStrongWizard:Main-d'oeuvre : maintien en poste, formation
  ou embauche > Je dois former ou perfectionner mes employés actuels + Je
  dois recruter et embaucher de nouveaux travailleurs > Québec > De 1 à 5
  millions de dollars > Foresterie et bois d'oeuvre
