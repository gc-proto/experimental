WHAT THIS IS
------------
A standalone, worker-facing companion to the employer wizard. It asks up to
four questions and shows the Employment Insurance, job, training, student
aid and apprenticeship supports that fit the answers. People who say they
are self-employed are handed off to the business wizard at question 1.

The page shell is the Canada Strong business wizard: same WET/GCWeb chrome,
same wb-fieldflow plugin, same click-analytics pattern, so the two tools
look and behave alike. Everything inside <main> is new. A <base> element
makes the shell's relative asset and link URLs resolve against canada.ca
when the file is opened from disk.

All thirteen supports listed under "Worker supports" on the source page are
reachable. Every destination URL was checked and returns HTTP 200.


HOW THE MECHANISM WORKS
-----------------------
No bespoke routing code. Each question is a hidden <div> holding a prompt
and a <ul> of answers; fieldflow renders it as a radio or checkbox
fieldset. Each answer carries a list of actions:

    removeClass  un-hide a panel (by #id) or a set of list items (by .class)
    append       render the next question, named by #id

Each question also carries a "default" action list, which fieldflow runs
once per change before the selected answers' actions. It re-hides whatever
the questions below had revealed, so changing an earlier answer resets the
results cleanly.

Group classes, used for reset:
    wz-g        every result element        (question 1 hides all of these)
    wz-g-sit    situation-gated lines       (question 1 sets)
    wz-g-need   the three need panels       (question 2 sets)
    wz-g-ei     EI-gated lines              (question 3 sets)
    wz-g-trade  apprenticeship lines        (question 4 sets)

Value classes, set by an answer and used to gate individual list items:
    wz-sit-laidoff | -reduced | -working
      (there is no wz-sit-selfemp: that answer reveals its panel by id,
       because it is the only thing that answer shows)
    wz-ei-receiving | -applying | -no | -unsure
    wz-trade-app | -any

Results live in #wz-results, hidden until the last question on the path is
answered: question 4 for employees, question 1 for the self-employed
hand-off.


THE TREE
--------

  Q1 situation   4 answers, radio     ─ sets .wz-sit-*    ─> Q2
                 └─ "self-employed"  ───────────────────────> results
  Q2 needs       3 answers, CHECKBOX  ─ opens need panels ─> Q3
  Q3 EI status   4 answers, radio     ─ sets .wz-ei-*     ─> Q4
  Q4 trades      3 answers, radio     ─ sets .wz-trade-*  ─> results

Two path lengths. The self-employed answer ends at question 1 and hands off
to the business wizard. The other three run four questions:
3 x (2^3 - 1) x 4 x 3 = 252 combinations, plus the single self-employed
path. Two panels on the hand-off, three to seven on the rest.


QUESTION 1 — What best describes your situation?
------------------------------------------------
Block id : #question-1   (radio)
Default  : addClass hidden on ".wz-g" and on "#wz-results"

  laidoff  I have been laid off, or my job has ended
  reduced  My hours or shifts have been reduced
  working  I am still working, but my workplace or industry is affected
           -> removeClass .wz-sit-<key>, then append #q2-needs
  selfemp  I am self-employed, or I run a small business
           -> removeClass #p-selfemp, removeClass #wz-results
              no append: the wizard ends here

The prompt does not name a total, because the two paths are not the same
length. Questions 2 to 4 still say "of 4"; they only ever appear on the
employee path.

THE SELF-EMPLOYED HAND-OFF
  This tool is for employees. A self-employed person or business owner
  needs financing, liquidity, transformation and workforce measures, not
  Employment Insurance, so that answer skips questions 2 to 4 and reveals
  the results immediately with a single panel:

    #p-selfemp   "This tool is for employees"
                 Find support for your business   [the business wizard]
                 /en/campaign/canadastrong/find-support-for-your-business.html

  The standing "More options" panel stays on screen underneath it, so the
  businesses-and-industries page and 1 800 O-Canada are still one click
  away. #p-selfemp carries only the wizard link: businesses-and-industries
  is in the standing panel already, and listing it twice on a two-panel
  screen would be conspicuous.

  Because the answer reveals #wz-results directly, and question 1's default
  re-hides it, switching to any other answer tears the hand-off down and
  collapses the results as normal. Verified in-browser.

The other three answers own income support. They drive #p-situation-note,
which carries exactly one line, the one matching the answer. All three
point at Temporary EI Measures; what differs is what they tell you to do
about it:

  laidoff  "Apply as soon as your job ends. The temporary measures waive
            the one-week waiting period."
  reduced  "You may still qualify for EI while you are working reduced
            hours."
  working  "You cannot claim while you are working your normal hours. Read
            the measures now so you know what applies if your hours are
            cut."

Why income is not one of the question 2 options: it would have put the same
EI link on screen twice, once here and once in a need panel. Question 1
already tells us the person's income situation, so it answers it.


QUESTION 2 — What do you need help with?
-----------------------------------------
Block id : #q2-needs   (CHECKBOX, multi-select)
Prompt   : ends "Select all that apply."
Default  : addClass hidden on ".wz-g-need, .wz-g-ei, .wz-g-trade" and on
           "#wz-results"; append #q3-ei
Each answer: removeClass its panel only

  Finding another job                          -> #p-job
  Training or new skills                       -> #p-training
  Paying for school, or managing a student loan -> #p-school

Why the append sits in "default" and not on the answers: fieldflow runs a
question's "default" once per change event, but runs an answer's actions
once per selected answer. With the append on each answer, ticking three
boxes would have rendered question 3 three times. In "default" it renders
exactly once however many boxes are ticked. Verified in-browser.

Ticking or unticking a box re-runs the question, which tears questions 3
and 4 down and collapses the results. That is the same reset the radio
questions do, and it is correct, but it does mean adding a need late costs
the answers below it. Hence "Select all that apply" in the prompt.

  #p-job     Finding another job
             Job Bank: job postings and labour market information
             Intro notes that provinces and territories also deliver
             job-search and employment assistance services under the labour
             market agreements listed in the training results.

  #p-training  Training and new skills
             EI-funded Labour Market Development Agreements (LMDAs)
             Skills for Success
             Training Finder
             Skills and employment training for Indigenous people

             The Indigenous line is shown to everyone who asks about
             training, with wording that lets people self-select. The
             wizard asks nothing about identity.

  #p-school  Paying for school and managing student loans
             Canada Student Grants and Loans
             Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP)
             Intro points at the EI note below for anyone on EI, or about
             to be, who is thinking of enrolling.


QUESTION 3 — Are you receiving EI, or do you plan to apply?
------------------------------------------------------------
Block id : #q3-ei   (radio)
Default  : addClass hidden on ".wz-g-ei, .wz-g-trade" and on "#wz-results"
Each answer: removeClass .wz-ei-<key>, then append #q4-trades

  receiving  I am receiving EI now
  applying   I have applied, or I am about to apply
  no         I am not receiving EI and do not plan to apply
  unsure     I am not sure whether I qualify

Drives #p-ei-note, which carries one line, the one matching the answer:

  receiving  EI and courses or training programs  [link]
             "You may be able to keep receiving EI while you take an
             approved course or training program."
  applying   EI and courses or training programs  [link]
             "If you are thinking about training while your claim is
             active, get approval first: enrolling without it can interrupt
             your benefits."
  no         no link, advice only
             "Not everything here needs an active EI claim. Job Bank is
             open to everyone, and the labour market agreements fund
             employment and training supports delivered by your province or
             territory."
  unsure     no link, advice only
             "Read the temporary EI measures above before you decide not to
             apply: they changed who can qualify and for how long. Applying
             costs you nothing if the answer turns out to be no."

The last two lines carry no link on purpose. The links they would have used
are already on screen in the situation panel and the training panel, and a
second copy of a link two panels down reads as an error rather than as
emphasis.

This question is also the only route to "EI and courses or training
programs", which is correct: that page is only meaningful to someone on EI
or about to be.


QUESTION 4 — Are you an apprentice or working in a skilled trade?
------------------------------------------------------------------
Block id : #q4-trades   (radio)
Default  : addClass hidden on ".wz-g-trade"
Each answer: removeClass #p-trades and .wz-trade-<key>, then
             removeClass #wz-results   (this is what shows the results)
The "No" answer only reveals #wz-results.

  app  Yes, I am a registered apprentice in a Red Seal trade
       -> #p-trades with all three lines:
          Canada Apprentice Loan
          EI for apprentices
          Tax deductions and credits for tradespeople
  any  I work in a skilled trade, but I am not an apprentice
       -> #p-trades with one line:
          Tax deductions and credits for tradespeople
  no   No
       -> no trades panel

The two apprentice-only supports say "apprentice" in their eligibility, so
they are gated to that answer. The tax page covers tradespeople generally,
so both trade answers see it.


ALWAYS ON SCREEN
----------------
A standing "More options" panel with no hidden class, so it survives every
combination of answers:
  - Support for businesses and industries impacted by tariffs
  - "See every measure in one place" -> the source page
  - "Didn't find what you were looking for?" -> 1 800 O-Canada

This is also what makes the business-supports link reachable on every path,
including the self-employed hand-off, where it sits under the business
wizard link.


ONE SUPPORT, ONE PANEL
----------------------
Because question 2 is multi-select, up to seven panels can be on screen at
once, so every one of the thirteen supports is listed in exactly one panel.
Verified in-browser on the maximal path (all three needs ticked, receiving
EI, Red Seal apprentice): fifteen links visible, zero repeats. The
self-employed hand-off was checked the same way: four links, zero repeats.

That rule is what shaped several placements:
  - Job Bank sits only in #p-job, not in the standing panel.
  - The Indigenous training link sits only in #p-training.
  - The LMDA link sits only in #p-training; #p-job's intro sentence refers
    to it in prose instead of linking it again.
  - Business supports sit only in the standing panel; there is no separate
    self-employed panel.
  - The two EI-note lines that would have repeated a link carry advice
    instead.

ANALYTICS
---------
The shell's click-analytics script is reused, with PREFIX changed to
"ServCan:CanadaStrongWorkerWizard:" and the form's
data-gc-analytics-formname set to match, so the worker tool reports
separately from the business one.

The script detects the end of the branch from the DOM rather than from a
question count: fieldflow stamps data-wb-fieldflow-source onto every
fieldset it renders and leaves the hidden source <div> in the document, so
the script can look the question up and read its actions.

The test is per ANSWER, not per question. Question 1 mixes three answers
that continue to question 2 with one, "I am self-employed", that ends the
wizard there, so a question-level test would have missed the hand-off
entirely. The rendered control cannot be read directly, because fieldflow
moves the actions off it and leaves a bind reference behind; instead the
script takes the control's position among its fieldset's inputs and reads
the source <li> at the same position, fieldflow having rendered them in
document order. A question that carries its own append, which is how a
multi-select question has to do it, is never the end of a branch.

A multi-select question contributes one entry, its ticked answers joined
with " + "; questions are joined with " > ". Both captured in-browser:

  ServCan:CanadaStrongWorkerWizard:I have been laid off, or my job has
  ended > Finding another job + Training or new skills + Paying for school,
  or managing a student loan > I am receiving EI now > Yes, I am a
  registered apprentice in a Red Seal trade

  ServCan:CanadaStrongWorkerWizard:I am self-employed, or I run a small
  business

Each stamp sits on the control that completed its own branch, so the two
path lengths report separately and the hand-off is measurable: how many
people land in the wrong tool is worth knowing.


VERIFICATION PERFORMED
----------------------
- Every fieldflow #id and .class selector resolves to an element that
  exists; no orphan selectors.
- All thirteen supports present; all fifteen outbound URLs return HTTP 200.
- Walked in a browser with the live WET/GCWeb assets:
  * the self-employed hand-off - one fieldset only, results shown straight
    away, two panels, four links, zero repeats, and the analytics stamp
    carrying the single answer;
  * switching from self-employed to "laid off" - hand-off panel hidden,
    results collapsed, question 2 rendered, and the four-question path then
    completing and stamping its own control;
  * maximal path, all three needs ticked - seven panels, fifteen links,
    zero repeated links;
  * minimal path, one need ticked, no EI, no trade - four panels;
  * self-employed path with the "applying" EI note and the non-apprentice
    trade answer - #p-trades correctly down to its single tax line;
  * multi-select appending question 3 exactly once when a second box is
    ticked;
  * re-answering question 1 after finishing - questions 2 to 4 torn down,
    results collapsed;
  * analytics firing with the multi-select path.


NOTES AND OPEN ITEMS
--------------------
- The source page has an "Apply for EI" link commented out in its own HTML,
  so it is not a live support and is not used here. A worker wizard that
  ends at "read about the temporary measures" without an apply route is a
  gap worth raising with the page owner rather than papering over with an
  invented link.
- Question 3 asks about EI status, not eligibility, and no answer tells
  anyone whether they qualify. The "unsure" path deliberately pushes people
  to read the measures and apply rather than self-screening out.
- The Training Finder URL carries the source page's own utm campaign
  parameters, kept verbatim. If this tool ships, it should get its own
  utm_source so its referrals can be told apart from the page's.
- The header language toggle still points at the live French business
  wizard, inherited from the shell. It needs re-pointing when a French
  worker wizard exists.
- "Question N of 4" is hard-coded in the prompts, as on the live tools. The
  analytics script does not depend on it. Question 1 names no total, since
  the self-employed answer makes the path length vary.
- The hand-off is a one-way door by design: someone who picks self-employed
  and then realises they are asking as an employee has to change their
  question 1 answer, which is one click. If testing shows people get stuck
  there, the panel could gain a "no, I am an employee" link that simply
  re-focuses question 1.
