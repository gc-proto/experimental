QUESTIONS AND LOGIC ADDED FOR EMPLOYER SUPPORTS
===============================================

Files : eric/canada-strong-employer-wizard.html       (English)

Date  : 2026-09-10 (revision 3)

This document covers only the employer path. The financing, liquidity,
transformation and "All of the above" paths are unchanged from the live
pages and are not repeated here.

The two language versions share one structure: same question ids, same
panel ids, same classes, same fieldflow actions, same analytics script.
Everything below therefore describes both. Where a French string matters -
a programme name, a URL, an answer label - it is given alongside the
English one. French wording is taken from the French Government of Canada
pages, not back-translated from the English mockup; see FRENCH SOURCES at
the foot of this document.


HOW THE WIZARD MECHANISM WORKS
------------------------------
The page uses WET's wb-fieldflow plugin. There is no bespoke routing code.

- Every question is a hidden <div> holding a <p> prompt and a <ul> of
  answers. fieldflow renders it as a radio or checkbox fieldset.
- Each answer <li> carries a data-wb-fieldflow list of actions:
    removeClass  un-hide a result panel (by #id) or a set of list items
                 (by .class)
    append       render the next question, named by #id
- Each question <div> carries a "default" action list. fieldflow runs it
  once per change, before the selected answers' actions, and it re-hides
  everything the questions below had revealed, so changing an earlier
  answer cleanly resets the results.
- Results stay inside #wz-results, which is itself hidden until the last
  question of the branch is answered.

Group classes used for reset:
    wz-g        every result element (question 1 hides all of these)
    wz-g-agn    sector-agnostic panels
    wz-g-rda    the regional development agency line
    wz-g-sz     size-conditional elements
    wz-g-sec    sector panels and hubs
    wz-g-emp    NEW - the employer situation panels
    wz-emp-size-*  NEW - employer size gates, kept separate from the
                   existing wz-sz-size-* so the employer size panel cannot
                   appear in the financing/liquidity/transformation results

QUESTION 2 (NEW) — What is your workforce situation right now?
--------------------------------------------------------------
Block id : #q2-need-emp
Renders  : CHECKBOXES ("renderas":"checkbox"). Prompt ends "Select all
           that apply."
Default  : addClass hidden on ".wz-g-emp, .wz-g-rda, .wz-g-sz, .wz-g-sec"
           and on "#wz-results"
           append #q3-need-emp

Why the append is in "default" and not on the answers: fieldflow runs a
question's "default" once per change event, but runs an answer's actions
once per selected answer. With the append on each answer, ticking three
boxes would have rendered question 3 three times. In "default" it renders
exactly once however many boxes are ticked. Verified in-browser: two boxes
ticked produces three fieldsets, not four.

Ticking or unticking a box re-runs the question, which tears questions 3
to 5 down and collapses the results - the same reset the radio questions
already do.

This is the question that does the routing. Each ticked box opens one
panel, and several panels can be on screen at once.

A. "I need to reduce employee hours instead of laying people off"
   reveals #p-emp-reduce
   Panel: Cutting hours instead of cutting jobs
     Work-Sharing Program                                        ESDC
       /en/employment-social-development/services/work-sharing.html
     Worker Retention Grant
       (requires an approved Work-Sharing agreement)             ESDC
       /en/employment-social-development/services/funding/
         worker-retention-grant.html
   Why: this is the situation Work-Sharing exists for, and the situation
   WRRP is being built to replace it with. The lead sentence carries the
   WRRP page's "up to $1,000 per participating employee" figure; the WRRP
   link itself is in the always-on panel above.

B. "I already have a Work-Sharing agreement in place"
   reveals #p-emp-agreement
   Panel: You already have a Work-Sharing agreement
     Worker Retention Grant                                      ESDC
     Training options while on Work-Sharing
       (Training Finder and other resources)                 Job Bank
       https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/career-planning/resources/
         training-options-work-sharing-employers
     Work-Sharing Program: agreement terms and flexibilities     ESDC
   Why: the Worker Retention Grant and the Job Bank training-options page
   both name an existing Work-Sharing agreement as their entry condition,
   so this answer promotes them instead of burying them behind a
   prerequisite note. Lead sentence carries the new 4-to-152-week range
   and the uneven hour-sharing flexibility from the WRRP page.

C. "I need to train or upskill my current employees"
   reveals #p-emp-train
   Panel: Training and upskilling your current employees
     WRRP: up to $1,000 per participating employee               ESDC
     Workforce Tariff Response - EI-funded LMDAs                 ESDC
       /en/employment-social-development/programs/training-agreements/
         workforce-tariff-response.html
     Training options for employers (Training Finder)        Job Bank
   Why: training money first, training tools second. Lead sentence
   explains the WRRP mechanic - training happens in the non-working hours.

D. "I need to recruit and hire new workers"
   reveals #p-emp-hire
   Panel: Recruiting and hiring new workers
     Adapt to today's marketplace using Job Bank:
       find, hire and retain workers                         Job Bank
       https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/hiring/resources
     Find workers with the right skills:
       available workers by occupation                       Job Bank
       https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/hiring/dashboard-available-workers
     Student Work Placement Program:
       wage subsidies to hire post-secondary students             ESDC
       /en/employment-social-development/services/
         student-work-placements-wage-subsidies.html
   Why: this is the only answer that leads with the two Job Bank hiring
   tools, which had no route into the live wizard at all.

E. "I have laid off workers or expect to soon"
   reveals #p-emp-layoff
   Panel: If you have laid off workers or expect to
     Support for workers impacted by tariffs               FIN / ESDC
     Work-Sharing Program: avoid further layoffs                ESDC
     Job Bank hiring resources: rehiring, redeployment and
       employment standards                                 Job Bank
   Why: an employer past the point of prevention needs the worker-facing
   hub first (so they can point staff at it), then the tools to stop the
   next round and to rehire.

Keeping C and D apart. In the first draft these read "I need to retrain or
upskill the workers I have" and "I need to find, hire or keep the right
workers", which overlapped on both retention and skills. The split is now
by who the worker is: C is about the people already on the payroll, D is
about people not yet hired. "Keep" left D, since retention is what A and B
are about. Workforce Tariff Response also left D - it had been listed
there as training for new hires - so training programmes now sit only in
C, and D is purely recruitment.

Overlap between panels, now that several can show at once. Each programme
is listed only where it is genuinely the answer:
  Work-Sharing        A, B (terms), E (avoid further layoffs)
  Worker Retention    A, B
  WRRP                always-on panel, plus C for the $1,000
  Job Bank training   B, C
  Job Bank hiring     D, E
The one duplicate a plausible pair of tick-boxes can produce is Worker
Retention Grant, if an employer ticks both A and B - which are close to
mutually exclusive in practice.

CLICK-ANALYTICS LOGIC
---------------------
The page's own script stamps the full answer path onto the control that
completes the wizard. It had assumed every branch was four radio
questions, which a five-question branch with a multi-select question
breaks. Rewritten to:

  End of branch  fieldflow puts data-wb-fieldflow-source="<source div id>"
                 on each fieldset it renders and leaves the hidden source
                 <div> in the document. The script looks the source up and
                 treats the question as the end of its branch when neither
                 the question config nor any of its answers contains an
                 "append" action. No hard-coded question count, and no list
                 of terminal questions to maintain.
  Answers        walks the fieldsets in order and joins each question's
                 checked controls, so a multi-select question contributes
                 one entry. Answers within a question join with " + ",
                 questions join with " > ".
  Guard          bails if any question on screen has no answer, which is
                 what happens if an employer unticks every box.

Paths produced, both captured in-browser:

  ServCan:CanadaStrongWizard:Liquidity for a tariff-driven cash-flow gap >
  Quebec > Larger enterprise ($150 million or more) > Steel, aluminium and
  copper

  ServCan:CanadaStrongWizard:Workforce: keeping, retraining or hiring
  workers > I need to reduce employee hours instead of laying people off +
  I need to recruit and hire new workers > British Columbia > Under $1
  million > Agriculture, agri-food, fish and seafood


THE "ALL OF THE ABOVE" PATH
---------------------------

(#ap-workforce-agnostic) was widened to carry all nine employer supports
rather than the four it held before. No logic change - the panel is still
revealed by the "All of the above" answer to question 1 through the
.wz-apagn class.
