Find support if tariffs have affected your job

Answer four questions and we will show you the Employment Insurance, job, training and student aid supports that fit your situation. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

Tell us about your situation

Question 1: What best describes your situation?

  • I have been laid off, or my job has ended
  • My hours or shifts have been reduced
  • I am still working, but my workplace or industry is affected
  • I am self-employed, or I run a small business

Question 2 of 4: What do you need help with? Select all that apply.

  • Finding another job
  • Training or new skills
  • Paying for school, or managing a student loan

Question 3 of 4: Are you receiving Employment Insurance (EI), or do you plan to apply?

  • I am receiving EI now
  • I have applied, or I am about to apply
  • I am not receiving EI and do not plan to apply
  • I am not sure whether I qualify

Question 4 of 4: Are you an apprentice or working in a skilled trade?

  • Yes, I am a registered apprentice in a Red Seal trade
  • I work in a skilled trade, but I am not an apprentice
  • No

Supports that match your answers

Income support for your situation

Employment Insurance is the main income support while you are not working, or working reduced hours.

  • Temporary Employment Insurance (EI) Measures (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    Apply as soon as your job ends. The temporary measures waive the one-week waiting period.
  • Temporary Employment Insurance (EI) Measures (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    You may still qualify for EI while you are working reduced hours.
  • Temporary Employment Insurance (EI) Measures (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    You cannot claim while you are working your normal hours. Read the measures now so you know what applies if your hours are cut.

Use the business support tool instead

If you are self-employed or you run a business, use the business tool instead.

  • Find support for your business: answer a few questions (Opens destination in a new tab.) Service Canada
    The business tool asks about your need, region, revenue and sector, and about your workforce if you have employees.

Finding another job

Your province or territory also delivers job-search and employment assistance services under the labour market agreements listed in the training results.

  • Job Bank: job postings and labour market information (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
  • Resources for job seekers: searching, resumes, applications and interviews (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
    Advice for each step of a job search, plus employment centres offering in-person help near you.

Training and new skills

  • EI-funded Labour Market Development Agreements (LMDAs) (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    Investments to help workers affected by tariffs access training and employment support.
  • Skills for Success: assess your skills and see what today's jobs ask for (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Training Finder: courses from recognized Canadian institutions (Opens destination in a new tab.) Job Bank
  • Skills and employment training for Indigenous people (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    Training and job support delivered by Indigenous service organizations, for First Nations, Inuit and Métis workers.

Paying for school and managing student loans

If you are on EI, or about to be, the EI note further down covers what you must do before you enrol in anything.

  • Canada Student Grants and Loans: full-time and part-time study (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP): help if you are struggling with loan payments (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC

About your EI situation

  • Employment Insurance (EI) and courses or training programs (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    You may be able to keep receiving EI while you take an approved course or training program.
  • Employment Insurance (EI) and courses or training programs (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    If you are thinking about training while your claim is active, get approval first: enrolling without it can interrupt your benefits.
  • Not everything here needs an active EI claim. Job Bank is open to everyone, and the labour market agreements fund employment and training supports delivered by your province or territory.
  • Read the temporary EI measures above before you decide not to apply: they changed who can qualify and for how long. Applying costs you nothing if the answer turns out to be no.

Apprenticeship and skilled trades

  • Canada Apprentice Loan: interest-free loans for Red Seal apprentices (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
  • EI for apprentices (Opens destination in a new tab.) ESDC
    Income support while you are on full-time technical training.
  • Tax deductions and credits for tradespeople (Opens destination in a new tab.) CRA
    Includes moving expenses, tuition fees and tool expenses.
