National Defence and Canadian Armed Forces Ombudsman (demo)

We work to ensure the Department of National Defence (DND) and the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) are fair in treating our  constituents. We are independent of the military chain of command and civilian management. 

  • We provide educational information. 
  • We address individual complaints and make recommendations to the DND and the CAF. 
  • We address systemic issues and make recommendations to the Minister.
