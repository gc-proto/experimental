New investments announced

In Spring 2026, the Government of Canada announced new national investments through the Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative, the Regional Tariff Response Initiative, the Build Communities Strong Fund Local Impact Stream, and a new Regional Development Fund to support the transformation of the forest sector.

PacifiCan will be delivering these programs in British Columbia. Further details will be shared when available; please consult the relevant program pages for more information.
